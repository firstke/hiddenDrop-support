Frequently Asked Questions

Does Hidden Drop need an internet connection?

No. Hidden Drop runs fully offline. There are no network requests, no account, no cloud sync, and no Game Center. Everything — gameplay, your best score, and settings — stays on your device.

How do I play Hidden Drop?

Hidden Drop is a falling-block puzzle with a twist: the bottom of the board is covered in fog. Standard blocks still fall, move left/right, rotate, and soft-drop using the four arrow buttons on the control bar. Fill complete horizontal lines to clear them and score.

What is the fog, and how does reveal work?

The lower part of the board is hidden in fog. The fog hides buried blocks that act as solid terrain — pieces lock on top of them, but you cannot see what is underneath. Tap the Tap button to enter reveal mode, then spend a limited number of taps on fog cells to clear the fog and expose what is below. Revealed blocks settle, can be cleared in full lines, and change how you plan your drops.

How many reveals do I get per level?

Level 1 gives you 4 reveals. Every later level gives you half the number of remaining fog cells at the start of the level. Reveal taps are limited on purpose: the core challenge is the trade-off between spending reveals to gain information and keeping on dropping and hoping the fog does not trap you.

How does leveling work?

When your score in the current level reaches the threshold (800 + (level − 1) × 500), a Level Up! prompt appears. Confirm it and the board refreshes with a denser fog, so the playable space changes and the game gets harder. Clearing more lines at once (a combo) scores more.

Where is my score saved, and can I reset it?

Your best score (BEST) is stored only on your device using iOS local storage, and is shown on the HUD (Fog / Score / Level). To start completely fresh, delete and reinstall the app — that clears the local best score and your music preference.

Does Hidden Drop request any permissions like microphone or camera?

No. The app requests no device permissions at all and shows no system permission prompts. Sound effects and background music are synthesized on your device with Apple's AVAudioEngine; we never use the microphone, and we do not record, store, or upload any audio.

Is an account or sign-in required?

No. There is no account, no sign-up, and no login. Every feature is available immediately after install.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads and no in-app purchases. Hidden Drop is a one-time purchase; everything is included and no further payment is ever required.

How do I pause, mute, or change the theme?

Use the top HUD buttons: tap the pause/play icon to pause or resume, tap the speaker icon to mute or unmute the background music, and the UI follows your system dark/light appearance. Your music preference is remembered on your device.

What are the system requirements?

Hidden Drop requires iOS 17.0 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad in portrait orientation (including upside-down).
